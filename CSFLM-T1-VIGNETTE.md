# CSFLM-T1-VIGNETTE

Tier 1. Written to be read aloud. No technical terms.

Status: to be written. It will follow one everyday clause and show what is said about it in three ways at once: what it is about, what the speakers are doing to each other, and how the message is put together. Those three answers, each looked at against the situation it is spoken in, make the nine numbers.
